# Records and nearby area (0.3.8)

Finished trip details show personal-record medals before completed stretches and collection rewards. They are derived from earlier completed walks, so reopening a trip still shows records earned on that walk even after a faster future trip. A first attempt establishes a baseline. Improvements below five seconds count as ties. All timing includes pauses. One medal is shown for each stretch's fastest record-breaking attempt on the trip. Record medals do not add XP; the existing lifetime milestones retain their XP rules.

A whole-route record must beat every earlier comparable complete walk, regardless of which ghost was selected or whether a ghost was used. Both tracks require usable start/end fixes within 30 seconds of the recording boundaries, no GPS gaps over 30 seconds, endpoints within 25 metres, at least 100 metres of route and lengths within ten percent. Eight-metre resampling and ordered proximity matching check that both complete paths follow the same geometry within 25 metres. This resolves overlapping outward/return passes using the finished tracks rather than the live matcher's latest state. GPS results remain approximate. Deleting reference walks or stretches can change recomputed records.

The former fixed home area becomes “Området rundt deg”. Only the selected radius is retained (1, 3 or 5 km; a valid old radius is migrated). Coordinates come from foreground location updates, are cleared when the app leaves the foreground, and expire without fresh fixes. Permission, loading and failure states are explicit; there is no silent fallback to an old home coordinate. Explicit map-data/goal requests obtain a fresh position with a timeout. Radius changes save immediately; expensive map downloads happen on request, not on every GPS update.

Active goals keep their original target set. New local goals use the position at request time and freeze an automatically resolved place name (or selection date) so they do not misleadingly change to a new “here” after travel. Historical trip-name suggestions use their actual discoveries, distance and time, never today's moving area.

Validation: domain tests cover chronology, ties, full-route completion, reversals, gaps, deviations, repeated laps and overlapping passes. `scripts/android-records-nearby-smoke.py` uses synthetic emulator data to check medals, the ghost explanation, migrated radius, missing permission, fresh position, relaunch and preservation of active goals. User GPS exports stay outside the repository and CI.
